Why the reference site doesn't set the scope

King of Cards is a full Shopify store: thousands of SKUs, nested category trees, cart, checkout, blog CMS, store locator, reviews. Bhagwati Card Company's brief is browse-and-WhatsApp with under 100 products — a smaller, cheaper build by design, not by cutting corners. Match the visual polish of the reference; don't match its feature list.

⚠ Any add-on checked pulls in the Node layer — quote it as one package, not per feature, since the server setup itself is a fixed cost regardless of how many add-ons sit on top of it.
